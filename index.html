Three tiers, because “free” is not one thing

A tool can be free in the sense that you will never pay for it, or free in the sense that you have not paid for it yet. Conflating those two is how a directory ends up recommending a 5-credit trial as an alternative to a $20 subscription.

Free forever

The funding does not come from you and does not have an end date: ads, a vendor's own budget, a university grant, or your own hardware.

How to test it: ask who pays the inference bill next month. If the answer is an advertiser or the vendor, it survives.

Limited free

A genuine free allowance that runs out: monthly credits, a one-time grant, an unpublished quota, or a “promotional period” the vendor can end.

How to test it: find the reset. If the allowance is one-time, or the number is unpublished, treat it as a trial.

Reference

Not a tool. Directories that probe their own rows, and student bundles that need verification to unlock.

How to test it: follow the evidence link, not the headline count.

Six questions the product page will not answer

  1. Who reads my prompts?

    The answers differ enormously. free-pi saves sessions as traces and may sell them; Freebuff says messages and traces may train ad models; VibeFree's classifier reads the first 1,500 characters and keeps only topic labels; idlepay reads one timestamp. Read this before pasting work code.

  2. What unit is the allowance in?

    Hours (Freebuff, 30/day), agent time (Tenzy, 20h 35m per cycle), credits per turn (Clixad, 60,000/day), multiples of a base rate (free-pi), responses per 5 hours (Autohand, 15). Two tools quoting “a daily allowance” can differ by fifty times.

  3. Is the free model the good model?

    Usually not. Freebuff's free tier is DeepSeek V4.1 Flash “Slow”; ADCode's free AI is partner models; DeepAI's free chat excludes Genius. Antigravity is the exception that puts Claude and Gemini on a $0 plan.

  4. What happens at the limit?

    Three shapes: it tells you when access resets (freecode), the day's allowance is simply gone (Freebuff, Tenzy), or it invites you onto an offerwall or a card (Clixad, ADCode).

  5. Can it be taken away?

    Gemini CLI's free tier ran at 1,000 requests a day, was cut to Flash-only in March 2026, and was switched off on 18 June 2026. Prefer tools that are Apache-2.0 or MIT — Freebuff, ADCode, OB-1, free-pi, Clixad — so a promise is not the only thing holding your workflow up.

  6. Does it need a card, an account, or both?

    None of the 13 free-forever entries needs a card. Accounts still gate some of them: GitHub for freecode, Google for Antigravity, AWS for Kiro, and a 30-day-old GitHub account for Autohand's free plan.

How this was checked, and what was not

What I did

  • Fetched each vendor's own landing page, pricing page or docs page on 6 November 2026 and read the offer, the funding statement, the limits and the FAQ text as published. Pricing pages were preferred over marketing pages wherever one existed — that is where the real numbers live.
  • Read the privacy and consent docs rather than the summary. free-pi's “may be shared or sold to third parties” appears only in the consent screen and privacy page, not the landing page.
  • Where a vendor's own page did not state a limit, the entry is marked directory and cites a directory that probes its rows on a schedule — awesome-free-ai-coding, freellm.site, VibeLedger.
  • Ignored blog listicles and review sites as evidence. Several rank first for these queries and contradict the vendors on billing and account requirements.

What I did not do

  • I did not install the CLIs or create accounts, so per-turn allowances, latency and model quality are unmeasured. Where a number appears, it is the vendor's number.
  • I did not benchmark coding ability. Nothing here is a quality ranking.
  • Free tiers change without notice. Every entry carries the date it was read — re-check the linked sources before relying on any figure.
  • No entry is a client of mine, and there are no affiliate or referral links. The freepi.ai and idlepay links you sent carried tracking parameters; those were stripped.

The one rule that survives all of this

Every tool here processes your prompt somewhere you do not control. Keep credentials out of prompts, and treat company code as a decision your employer makes, not one a free plan makes for you. Free is a fair trade as long as you can name what you handed over.
